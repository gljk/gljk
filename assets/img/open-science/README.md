# Open science badge artwork

The Open Data, Open Materials, and Preregistered SVGs in this directory are
the Center for Open Science's Badges to Acknowledge Open Practices, created by
David Mellor and vectorized by Ainali and AntiCompositeNumber. They are used
without modification under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Source: <https://osf.io/tvyxz/> (mirrored on Wikimedia Commons).

The Open Code SVG is the [CoMSES Net vector badge](https://www.comses.net/resources/open-code-badge/),
derived from the Center for Open Science badge project and likewise licensed
under CC BY 4.0.
