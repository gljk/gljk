Mortality in Serbia and the European Union: A Comparative Analysis

Marko Galjak

Master's thesis, University of Belgrade, Faculty of Geography 2014

Abstract

This thesis compares mortality conditions in Serbia with those in the European Union using life expectancy, age- and cause-specific mortality, standardized mortality, and amenable mortality. It examines differences in epidemiological transition, cardiovascular disease, cancer, infant and maternal mortality, and health-system effectiveness.
